Founder

B. G. Raghupathy

A first-generation entrepreneur whose defining conviction was that India’s most demanding energy infrastructure projects could be executed with home-grown talent, backed by the right high-quality global technology partnerships.

Milestones

1985 to today

  1. 1985

    Incorporated as a JV with GEA Energietechnik GmbH, Germany, becoming a manufacturer of high-tech capital equipment, followed by multiple global partnerships and technical collaborations.

  2. 2000

    Pioneers the BOP turnkey framework in India and enters power as a full turnkey EPC contractor, with extensive in-house engineering.

  3. 2000 – 2012

    India adds over 60,000 MW of thermal capacity; BGR is one of the largest private-sector contributors, at roughly 25% of thermal EPC at peak, behind only BHEL.

  4. 2008

    IPO on NSE and BSE, subscribed about 120 times at a 75% premium, with a market cap of about Rs 3,500 Cr; secures large EPC contracts from RRVUNL and TANGEDCO.

  5. 2010

    Two JVs with Hitachi, BGR Turbines (steam turbines and generators) and BGR Boilers (supercritical steam generators); key projects include NTPC Solapur and Meja (4×660 MW boilers) and NTPC Lara (2×800 MW turbine and generator).

  6. Today

    New growth engines defined and early market-entry work underway.
